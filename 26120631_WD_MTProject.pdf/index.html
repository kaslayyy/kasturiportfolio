About Me

A person, not a LinkedIn headline.

I'm a BBA student at IIM Bangalore (Digital Business & Entrepreneurship), interested in financial markets, investing, and personal trading. But numbers are only one part of the story. I'm just as fascinated by the psychology behind decisions, the ideas that grow into businesses, and the little things that influence how the world works.

I like connecting dots between seemingly unrelated ideas, falling down interesting research rabbit holes, questioning the obvious, and exploring subjects I never planned to be curious about. Essentially, if there's something interesting to figure out, I'm probably already looking into it.
